Apogee · TI-84 Plus CE

Calculator programs

Notes and step-by-step solvers for AP Physics 1. Choose what the question asks you to find, choose the situation, type the numbers it asks for, and it shows the full work and answer.

How the programs work

  1. FIND WHAT? Pick what the question asks for (speed, distance, force…).
  2. What happens? Pick the situation (dropped, pulled across the floor, on a ramp…).
  3. Type the numbers. Every prompt shows its unit. Type 0 for anything at rest or not given when the prompt says so.
  4. Work and answer. The work screen opens by itself: the givens, the equation, your numbers plugged in, and the answer. Scroll with the up and down arrows. Press CLEAR when you're done, then pick See work again, Continue or Main menu.

Each program also has Hidden values (clue words like "from rest" and unit conversions), Notes slides for the whole unit, and a Custom equation solver for questions that don't fit a menu.

Install

  1. Install TI Connect CE (free from Texas Instruments) on a computer.
  2. Connect the calculator with its USB cable and turn it on.
  3. Drag the .8xp files onto the calculator in TI Connect CE. When asked where to send them, choose RAM.
  4. On the calculator press prgm, pick the program, and press enter twice.
If something goes wrongERR:ARCHIVED: press 2nd + (MEM), choose 2 (Mem Management), then Prgm, select the program and press enter to unarchive it. ERR:SYNTAX with toString: update the calculator to OS 5.2 or newer with TI Connect CE. To stop a running program press on.
ValuesThe programs use g = 10 m/s². To use 9.8, pick Set G from the menu (under More in the Unit 1 and Unit 2 programs).
