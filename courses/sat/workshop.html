The SAT workshop

Timed sprints and grid-ins that feel like the real digital SAT. The clock is part of the training.

Mission 1Grid-in warm-up

Student-produced responses: no answer choices, just type the number. Fractions are fine.

Mission 2Reading and Writing sprint

Eight short Reading and Writing questions in six minutes. One question per passage, just like the digital SAT.

Mission 3Algebra sprint

Eight algebra and advanced-math questions in eight minutes. Type your answers.

Mission 4Data, geometry and trig

Six problem-solving and geometry questions. Type exact answers.

Mission 5Mini module

Ten mixed questions, twelve minutes. Pace yourself: about 70 seconds each.
