4.1Linear equations in one variable

Isolate the variable. But on the SAT, often you don't need the variable at all: look for the expression they ask about.

No solution

Same x-coefficient, different constants: 3x + 2 = 3x + 5 is impossible.

Infinitely many

Both sides identical after simplifying: 2(x + 1) = 2x + 2.
Worked example: For what value of k does 5x + k = 5x − 2 have infinitely many solutions? For what values does it have none?
Infinitely many when k = −2 (sides identical). No solution for any other k.

4.2Linear functions and graphs

f(x) = mx + b: m is the rate of change (slope), b is the starting value (y-intercept). Word problems love asking what m or b means in context.

Worked example: C(m) = 3.5m + 25. What does 3.5 mean?
Each additional gigabyte of data adds $3.50 to the monthly cost. (25 is the cost with zero data.)

From two points: slope = Δy/Δx. Parallel lines share a slope; perpendicular slopes multiply to −1. From a table: check the slope between any two rows.

4.3Systems of linear equations

Equations look like…Solutions
different slopesexactly one
same slope, different interceptnone (parallel)
one is a multiple of the otherinfinitely many (same line)
Worked example: For what value of c does the system 3x + 2y = 7 and 6x + cy = 10 have no solution?
No solution means parallel: the left sides must be proportional, 6/3 = c/2 → c = 4 (and 10 ≠ 14, so not the same line).

4.4Inequalities and linear models

Same moves as equations, but flip the sign when you multiply or divide by a negative. Word cues: "at least" ≥, "at most" / "no more than" ≤, "more than" >.

Worked example: A van can carry at most 1,200 kg. The driver weighs 80 kg and each box weighs 35 kg. What's the most boxes it can carry?
80 + 35b ≤ 1200 → 35b ≤ 1120 → b ≤ 32. So 32 boxes.

If that readout is ≥ 0, the point (2, 1) satisfies y ≤ mx + b.
