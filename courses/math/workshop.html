The graphing workshop

Match target graphs by dragging sliders, find exact values without a calculator, and race an algebra sprint. If you can match a graph, you understand its equation.

Mission 1Match the line

Drag the sliders until your blue line sits exactly on the dashed orange target.

Mission 2Match the parabola

Vertex form: y = a(x − h)² + k. Find a, h and k.

Mission 3Match the zeros

Factored form: y = a(x − p)(x − q). The zeros are where the graph crosses the x-axis.

Mission 4Match the exponential

y = a · bˣ. a is the starting value at x = 0, b is the growth factor per step.

Mission 5Match the wave

y = A sin(B(x − C)) + D. Amplitude, frequency, shift, midline.

Mission 6Match the logarithm

y = a · ln(x − h) + k. The vertical asymptote is at x = h.

Mission 7Exact values

No calculator. Type exact numbers (fractions are fine, e.g. 1/2).

Mission 8Algebra sprint

Eight quick algebra questions in four minutes. Type numbers.
