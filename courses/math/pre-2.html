Before Precalc · Unit 2

Lines and linear equations

Solving equations, slope, the three forms of a line, systems, and inequalities.

2.1Solving linear equations

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. Undo operations in reverse order: first clear addition/subtraction, then multiplication/division.

FractionsIf the equation has fractions, multiply every term by the common denominator first. x/2 + x/3 = 5 → 3x + 2x = 30 → x = 6.

2.2Slope and the three forms of a line

Slope is rise over run: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). It's how much y changes every time x goes up by 1.

FormEquationBest for
Slope-intercepty = mx + bgraphing: start at b, then rise m per 1 run
Point-slopey − y₁ = m(x − x₁)writing a line from a point and a slope
StandardAx + By = Cintercepts: set x = 0, then y = 0

Parallel lines

Same slope. y = 2x + 1 and y = 2x − 5 never meet.

Perpendicular lines

Slopes are negative reciprocals: 2 and −1/2. Their product is −1.
Worked example: Write the line through (2, 5) and (6, −3).
Slope = (−3 − 5)/(6 − 2) = −8/4 = −2. Point-slope: y − 5 = −2(x − 2). Slope-intercept: y = −2x + 9.

2.3Systems of equations

A system's solution is the point where the lines meet. Two lines can meet once, never (parallel), or everywhere (same line).

Substitution vs eliminationIf one variable is already alone (y = …), substitute. If coefficients line up or are easy to make opposite, eliminate.

2.4Inequalities and absolute value

Solve inequalities like equations, with one extra rule: multiplying or dividing by a negative flips the sign. −2x < 6 → x > −3.

Unit summary

Equations

Distribute, collect x's on one side, undo + −, then × ÷. Always check.

Slope

m = Δy/Δx. Parallel: same m. Perpendicular: m₁·m₂ = −1.

Systems

Solution = intersection. Substitute or eliminate. Parallel → none, same line → infinitely many.

Inequalities

Flip the sign when you × or ÷ by a negative. |x − c| < d → within d of c.
