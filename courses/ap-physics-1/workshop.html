The physics workshop

Launch projectiles at targets, smash carts together and race the clock. Predict with physics first and you'll hit it in one shot.

Mission 1Hit the target

The launcher is locked at 45°. Choose the launch speed that lands the ball in the green zone.

Mission 2Over the wall

A 12 m wall stands 20 m away. Clear it and land in the target 38–42 m away.

Mission 3Off the cliff

Now you're on a 20 m cliff and the speed is fixed at 12 m/s. Find an angle that lands in the 27.3–28.5 m zone.

Mission 4Sticky carts

Cart 1 (2 kg) moves at 6 m/s into a cart at rest; they stick. Choose the second cart's mass so they move off together at exactly 2 m/s.

Mission 5Stop dead

An elastic collision: cart 1 (3 kg, 5 m/s) hits a cart at rest. Choose the second mass so cart 1 stops completely.

Mission 6Where did the energy go?

Cart 1 (4 kg, 5 m/s) hits a cart at rest and they stick. Choose the second mass so exactly 30 J of kinetic energy is lost.

Mission 7Quick calculations

Seven numbers to find. Type the answer (fractions like 3/4 are fine). Use g = 10 m/s².

Mission 8Concept sprint

Eight conceptual questions against the clock. No calculator needed — think about the physics.
