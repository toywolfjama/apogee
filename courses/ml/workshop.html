The model-training workshop

Fit lines by hand, tune learning rates, draw decision boundaries, beat overfitting and train a neural network. Every mission has a goal you can check live.

Mission 1Fit it by eye

Use only the w and b sliders (no gradient descent) to get the mean squared error below 0.35.

Mission 2Let the math do it

Start from w = 0, b = 0 and let gradient descent fit the line. Get the MSE below 0.5 in 60 steps or fewer by choosing a good learning rate.

Mission 3The perfect step size

On the smooth bowl, choose a learning rate that lands exactly at the bottom (w = 3) in at most 2 steps.

Mission 4Escape the local minimum

On the bumpy curve, find the lowest valley (loss below 1.25) and settle there.

Mission 5Be the classifier

Drag the handles to draw a straight line that separates the blue and orange points with at least 95% accuracy.

Mission 6Curved boundaries

Train logistic regression on the Circle data to at least 90% accuracy.

Mission 7Catch every case

A disease screening test must catch at least 98% of sick patients. Move the threshold to reach recall ≥ 0.98 (keep the model's separation at 1.40).

Mission 8Tame the wiggles

Use a degree-9 (or higher) polynomial but keep the test error below 0.08.

Mission 9Solve XOR with a neural network

Train a network with at most 3 hidden neurons to 97% accuracy on the XOR data.

Mission 10Let the clusters find themselves

Pick the right k and run k-means until it converges on the best clustering (no center stuck between two blobs).

Mission 11Attention by hand

Five quick calculations from the transformer and embeddings unit.
