L'atelier

Match words, train your ear, take dictation and race through conjugations from the present to the subjunctive. Turn your sound on.

Mission 1Memory match

Flip two cards at a time and match each French word with its English meaning. You'll hear every French word you turn over.

Mission 2Écoute bien

Listen to each French word and choose what it means. Use “Play again” as often as you like.

Mission 3Dictée

Listen and type the French word. Accents count, but you get half credit if only an accent is wrong.

Mission 4Present tense sprint

Ten conjugations in the present tense. Type the verb form (the subject is optional).

Mission 5Passé composé

Helper verb + past participle. Watch for verbs that take être.

Mission 6Grammar check

Eight quick questions on articles, agreement and pronouns.

Mission 7Imparfait and futur

Mixed set: describe the past (imparfait) and predict the future (futur simple).

Mission 8Boss level: conditionnel and subjonctif

The two moods that separate intermediate from advanced French.

Mission 9Memory match: harder words

Same game, less common vocabulary.
