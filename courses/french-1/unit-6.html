À la maison

This unit is about home: the rooms and furniture, saying where things are, chores, and telling people what to do. You'll also learn the imperative, the verb form for commands and instructions.

In this unit

Prepositions that end in de combine with le and les: à côté du lit (de + le), près des fenêtres (de + les), but à côté de la porte and près de l'armoire stay the same.

Chez

chez + a person means at / to someone's place. Use stress pronouns after it: chez moi, chez toi, chez lui, chez elle, chez nous, chez vous, chez eux.

On va chez Marie ce soir ?Are we going to Marie's tonight?
Je rentre chez moi.I'm going home.

6.4The imperative: giving commands

Range ta chambre ! Allons-y ! Asseyez-vous.

The imperative has only three forms: tu, nous (let's…) and vous. Take the present tense and drop the subject.

-er: ranger-ir: finir-re: attendre
(tu)Range !Finis !Attends !
(nous)Rangeons !Finissons !Attendons !
(vous)Rangez !Finissez !Attendez !

The only trick: -er verbs (and aller) drop the final s in the tu form: tu ranges → Range !, tu vas → Va !

Veuillez fermer la porte.Please close the door. (very polite, on signs)

Negative commands

Wrap the verb as usual: Ne touche pas ! (Don't touch!), Ne parlez pas !

Reflexive verbs

Detailed explanationImperative details

Let's…

The nous form means "let's": Allons au cinéma ! (Let's go to the movies!) Mangeons ! (Let's eat!)

The s comes back before y and en

For sound, -er verbs keep their s before y or en: Vas-y ! (Go on!), Manges-en ! (Eat some!)

Being polite

A bare command can sound bossy. Add s'il te plaît / s'il vous plaît, or ask instead: Tu peux fermer la porte ? Pourriez-vous m'aider ?

Pronouns in commands

In positive commands, me and te become moi and toi after the verb: Aide-moi !, Assieds-toi ! In negative commands they stay before: Ne me regarde pas !

Common mistakes

Points students lose most often.

  1. Keeping the s in tu commands of -er verbs: Ranges ta chambre ! → Range ta chambre !
  2. Dropping the s in -ir / -re verbs: Fini ! → Finis !
  3. à côté de le lit → à côté du lit.
  4. Using the subject in a command: Tu mange ! → Mange !
  5. Lève-te ! → Lève-toi ! (te becomes toi after the verb).
  6. Ne lève-toi pas → Ne te lève pas (in the negative, the pronoun goes back in front).
  7. Saying premier étage for the ground floor: that's le rez-de-chaussée.
