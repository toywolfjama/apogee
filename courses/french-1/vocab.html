How to actually remember words

What works, and what doesn't.

  1. Learn nouns with their article. Not "maison" but une maison. The gender is part of the word.
  2. Say every word out loud after you hear it. Saying words aloud makes them stick much better than reading silently.
  3. Small batches, often. Ten words a day for ten days beats a hundred the night before a test. Use the 1–100, 101–200… ranges.
  4. Test yourself before you feel ready. Hide the English or take a 10-word quiz. Trying to remember is what makes a word stick.
  5. Come back to your misses. The quiz lists the words you got wrong; quiz those again the next day.
  6. Use the word in a sentence about you. J'ai besoin d'un nouveau téléphone. A word tied to your own life is easier to recall.
  7. Watch for cognates and false friends. Many words look like English: important, la musique, la date. But some don't mean what they look like: la librairie is a bookstore (a library is la bibliothèque), and actuel means current, not actual.
