Math part 1: algebra

Numbers and complex numbers, equations and formulas, functions, logs, sequences and word problems.

3.1Numbers and quantity

Fractions, decimals, percents

Convert freely: 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%.

Exponents and roots

a^m·a^n = a^(m+n), (a^m)^n = a^(mn), a^(−n) = 1/aⁿ, √(ab) = √a·√b.

Factors and multiples

LCM for "when do they line up again"; GCF for "largest equal groups."

Complex numbers

i = √−1, i² = −1. (3 + 2i)(1 − i) = 3 − 3i + 2i − 2i² = 5 − i.
Worked example: Bus A comes every 12 min, bus B every 18 min. Both just left. When together again?
LCM(12, 18) = 36 minutes.

3.2Algebra

Solve equations, inequalities, systems and absolute values; rearrange formulas; factor; work with quadratics.

When d < 0 there are no solutions: an absolute value is never negative.

3.3Functions

Notation

f(3) means plug 3 in. f(x) = 5 means find the input(s) that give 5.

Composition

f(g(x)): do g first.

Domain & range

No division by 0; no even root of a negative; logs need positive inputs.

Logarithms

log_b(a) = c means bᶜ = a. log 1000 = 3. log(xy) = log x + log y.

Sequences: arithmetic aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d; geometric aₙ = a₁·r^(n − 1).

3.4Word problems and modeling

  1. Name the variable in words ("x = number of adult tickets").
  2. Translate each sentence into an equation.
  3. Solve, then answer the question actually asked (often not x itself).
