4.2Trigonometry

SOH-CAH-TOA

Right triangles only.

Unit circle

(cos θ, sin θ). Know 30°, 45°, 60° exact values and the quadrant signs.

Identities

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; tan θ = sin θ/cos θ.

Law of Sines / Cosines

Non-right triangles: a/sin A = b/sin B; c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C. The ACT usually prints these in the question if needed.

4.3Statistics and probability

Center

Mean, median, mode. Adding a constant to every value shifts all three; multiplying scales them.

Spread

Range, standard deviation. Adding a constant doesn't change spread.

Counting

Multiply choices: 3 shirts × 4 pants = 12 outfits. Order matters → permutations; order doesn't → combinations: C(n, k) = n!/(k!(n − k)!).

Probability

P(A and B) = P(A)·P(B) if independent. P(not A) = 1 − P(A). Expected value = Σ value × probability.

4.4Matrices, vectors and other higher topics

Matrices

Add entry by entry (same size). Multiply rows × columns: (m×n)(n×p) gives m×p. Determinant of [[a, b], [c, d]] = ad − bc.

Vectors

⟨a, b⟩ + ⟨c, d⟩ = ⟨a + c, b + d⟩. Magnitude √(a² + b²).

Logs and exponentials

Solve 3ˣ = 20 with x = log 20 / log 3.

Ellipses & parabolas

x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 is an ellipse centered at the origin with x-intercepts ±a.
