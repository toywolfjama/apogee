ACT Science: read the data

The skills Science really tests: reading figures, experimental design, conflicting viewpoints and the new engineering questions.

6.1The four skills Science really tests

Read the figure

Check axes, units, legends. Many questions are just "find the value at x = 30."

Trends

As X increases, Y increases / decreases / peaks / levels off. Say it in words.

Interpolate / extrapolate

Between data points: estimate in between. Beyond them: continue the trend (carefully).

Experimental design

Independent variable = what the scientist changes. Dependent = what's measured. Controls = kept the same. A control group gets no treatment.
You don't need to read everythingGo straight to the question, find the figure it names, answer it. Read the text only when a question asks about methods or viewpoints.

Passage 3: conflicting viewpoints

In one summer, thousands of fish died in a lake. Two scientists propose explanations.

Scientist 1: Fertilizer from nearby farms washed into the lake after heavy rain, feeding a huge bloom of algae. When the algae died, bacteria decomposed it and used up most of the oxygen in the water. The fish suffocated. Oxygen readings near the lake bottom fell below 2 mg/L during the die-off.

Scientist 2: A record heat wave warmed the lake's surface water to 31°C. Warm water holds less dissolved oxygen, and many of the lake's fish species can't survive above 29°C. The die-off began the week the heat wave peaked, before any algae bloom was observed.

Questions: passage 3

Use the viewpoints above.
