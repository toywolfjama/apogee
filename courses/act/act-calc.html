Calculator tactics

Desmos on the digital ACT, TI-84 keystrokes for paper, regressions, stats, complex numbers and matrices.

1Which calculator will you have?

FormatCalculator
Digital ACT (online)A built-in Desmos graphing calculator on the Math section, plus your own permitted handheld if you want
Paper ACTOnly your own permitted calculator

Calculators are allowed on Math only. Calculators with a computer algebra system (CAS), like the TI-89 or TI-Nspire CAS, are banned; the TI-84 Plus CE is allowed. Always check ACT's current calculator policy before test day.

Desmos on the ACT starts in degreesThe ACT's Desmos defaults to degrees (desmos.com defaults to radians). If you practice on desmos.com, switch to degrees in the wrench menu so it matches.

2Desmos tactics for ACT Math

All the SAT Desmos tactics work on the digital ACT too. The ones that pay off most on ACT questions:

1Intersections and systems

Type both equations, click the crossing.

Type:y=3x-7 y=-x+5

2Regressions from a table

ACT loves "which equation fits this table."

Type:y_1 ~ mx_1+b y_1 ~ ab^{x_1}

3Complex numbers: do them by hand

Don't count on the test's Desmos for complex arithmetic. FOIL (a + bi)(c + di) with i² = −1; it takes seconds. A TI-84 in a+bi mode also works.

4Matrices

Desmos doesn't do matrices in the graphing calculator; compute the determinant ad − bc by hand or use a TI-84.

5Trig values

With degrees set, type the expression directly.

Type:sin(150) cos(60)^2+sin(60)^2

6Counting

Desmos has nCr and nPr and factorials.

Type:nCr(10,3) nPr(5,3) 6!

7Logs

Natural log and any base.

Type:log(1000) ln(e^3) log_2(32)

Type log, then _2 for the base.
